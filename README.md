# Big Three Playbook

**An AI development & alignment playbook — dedicated to the Big Three of
science fiction, informed by the people and companies shaping AI today,
and driven by original predictions about where AI is going.**

---

## What is this?

A playbook for developing AI with safety, transparency, and human
accountability built in — not bolted on. It turns the lessons of classic
science fiction into practical principles for modern AI development.

## Why does it exist?

The "Big Three" of science fiction — **Isaac Asimov, Arthur C. Clarke, and
Robert A. Heinlein** — imagined intelligent machines decades before they
existed. They asked the hard questions first: What happens when machines
can reason? Who stays accountable? What could go wrong?

Today's AI builders are answering those questions in real time, often
without reading the people who asked them first. This playbook connects
the two — the writers who imagined it and the builders who are making it
real — and adds original predictions about what comes next.

## Who is it for?

- **AI developers** who want alignment principles they can actually apply
- **Researchers and students** studying AI safety and ethics
- **Science fiction readers** interested in how the genre's ideas map to
  real technology
- **Anyone** thinking seriously about where AI is going

## A note on framing

Layers 2 and 3 include real, living people and companies. They are included
as **material to study**, not as endorsements. Layer 4 is clearly the
author's original view.
