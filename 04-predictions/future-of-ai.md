# Future of AI — Predictions

> **Note:** Everything on this page is the author's **prediction**, clearly
> labeled as such. These are not facts. They are reasoned guesses about where
> AI is heading, written to be revisited and revised over time.

## How to read this page

Each prediction has four parts:
- **Prediction** — what I think will happen
- **Timeframe** — when I expect it
- **Confidence** — low / medium / high
- **Reasoning** — why I think so

Predictions are dated. When reality proves one wrong, it gets updated — not deleted.

---

## Predictions

### 1. AI moves from "chat" to "agent"
- **Prediction:** The dominant interface shifts from conversational chatbots to
  autonomous agents that plan and execute multi-step tasks on a user's behalf.
- **Timeframe:** 2026–2028
- **Confidence:** High
- **Reasoning:** Models are already being wrapped with tool use, memory, and
  planning loops. The economics favor delegation over conversation. The
  bottleneck is reliability and trust, not capability.

### 2. Small, specialized models beat giant general ones for most tasks
- **Prediction:** Most production AI work shifts to small, fine-tuned, or
  distilled models running close to the user — not one giant model for everything.
- **Timeframe:** 2027–2030
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Cost, latency, privacy, and regulation all push toward local
  and specialized models. Giant models remain for frontier research and
  hard reasoning, but the "default" deployment becomes small.

### 3. AI alignment becomes an engineering discipline, not a philosophy
- **Prediction:** Alignment stops being a debate and becomes a stack of
  measurable engineering practices — evals, red-teaming, interpretability
  tooling, and deployment gates.
- **Timeframe:** 2026–2029
- **Confidence:** Medium-High
- **Reasoning:** Regulation and liability force measurability. "We tried our
  best" will not survive an audit. The field is already moving this way.

### 4. The "Big Three" of AI become infrastructure, not products
- **Prediction:** The largest AI labs become utilities — like cloud providers —
  with smaller companies building products on top.
- **Timeframe:** 2028–2032
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Compute and talent concentration favor a few labs. But
  product differentiation happens at the application layer. The pattern
  repeats what happened with cloud computing.

### 5. Human oversight becomes a legal requirement, not a best practice
- **Prediction:** High-stakes AI systems (medical, legal, financial, military)
  will require documented human-in-the-loop review by law in major economies.
- **Timeframe:** 2027–2031
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Reasoning:** Every high-stakes technology from aviation to medicine went
  through this transition. AI is on the same path. The question is timing,
  not direction.

### 6. Open-source AI closes the gap with closed frontier labs
- **Prediction:** Open-weight models will consistently trail closed frontier
  models by only a few months — not years.
- **Timeframe:** Ongoing, 2026 onward
- **Confidence:** Medium-High
- **Reasoning:** The open-source release cadence has accelerated dramatically.
  Each generation of open models narrows the gap. The trend has held for
  three years straight.
