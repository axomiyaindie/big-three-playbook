# Foundational Theory

> **Note:** These scientists built the theoretical foundations of modern AI —
> neural networks, deep learning, and the algorithms that make today'"'"'s models
> possible. Their work is *theory-first*: it made the applications possible.

- [Geoffrey Hinton](geoffrey-hinton.md) — "Godfather of Deep Learning," 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics
- [Yoshua Bengio](yoshua-bengio.md) — "Godfather of AI," 2018 Turing Award
- [Yann LeCun](yann-lecun.md) — "Godfather of AI," 2018 Turing Award, Meta Chief AI Scientist

Together, Hinton, Bengio, and LeCun are known as the **"Godfathers of AI"** —
the three winners of the 2018 Turing Award for deep learning.
